# Welcome to Reversed by Goni

This is the first post.

This site will be my technical notebook for cybersecurity work: reverse engineering, CTFs, mobile security, malware analysis, digital forensics, and offensive security research.

The goal is simple:

> Learn something difficult. Break it down. Rebuild it. Document it.

Future writeups will include commands, tooling, screenshots, mistakes, dead ends, and the actual reasoning behind the solution.

More importantly, this blog is going to evolve as the research does.
